This Privacy Policy explains how First Rep ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles your information. First Rep is a structured pull-up programme for iPhone that takes adults from no pull-ups to their first strict pull-up, then to five and ten, one numbered Day at a time.

The App is developed and operated by Nandha Kumar Ilangovan ("the Developer"), who is responsible for your information under this Policy.

First Rep is built so that your training stays yours. The App has no accounts, no sign-in, no cloud backend, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. Everything you log is stored on your iPhone and never sent to us. By downloading or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. The short version

  • On your iPhone only: your Days, logged sets, records, recovery sessions, placement answers, equipment and settings are stored on your device. We have no servers that receive them.
  • No account: you never create an account or give us your name or email to use the App.
  • No tracking: no IDFA, no App Tracking Transparency prompt, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no cross-app tracking.
  • Payments through Apple: subscriptions and free trials are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • Local reminders: notifications are scheduled on your device by iOS. We do not run a push notification server.
  • Erase anytime: You › Settings › Reset programme permanently erases all training data on your iPhone. Deleting the App also removes it.

2. Information the App stores on your device

To run your programme, the App keeps the following in its private storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS data protection. None of it is transmitted to the Developer.

  • Training records: each Day you complete or end early, the sets you log (reps, seconds held, the help you used such as a band, controlled lowering, and the strict-rep checklist), how the Day felt, and its start and end times.
  • Programme progress: your starting stage, stage tests passed, milestones reached, Days done, and the session you are currently in, so you can resume after closing the App.
  • Recovery and pauses: active-recovery routines you finish, and any pause you start, including a pause because something hurt.
  • Placement and equipment: your answers to the starting questions (for example, whether you can hang for 20 seconds) and the equipment you have (bar, band, chair).
  • Preferences: your reminder setting and time, voice coaching on or off, and which one-time tips and reveals you have already seen.

2.1 Safety check answers

During setup, the App asks a short safety check (age and a few health questions) to decide whether you should speak to a professional before training. These answers are used only on screen to let you continue. They are not stored or transmitted. If you stop a Day because something hurt, the App records only that a pain pause happened, on your device, so your plan can pause and ease you back in.

2.2 Device backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS may include the App's on-device data in that backup, as it does for most apps. Those backups are governed by Apple and your own settings, not by us. We cannot access them.

3. Subscriptions and purchases

First Rep offers auto-renewing subscriptions (First Rep Pro, annual and weekly) with free trials. Purchases are processed directly by Apple using StoreKit. The App checks your subscription status with Apple on your device to unlock Pro. We do not use a third-party purchase service, and we never receive your name, email, card or billing details. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App schedules local reminders on your iPhone: when your next Day is ready, when your stage test is next, a gentle reminder after a few quiet days, when a rest period ends during a session, and two days before a free trial ends. These are created and delivered by iOS on your device. No notification content is sent to us. You can turn them off in the App or in iOS Settings at any time.

5. Voice coaching, sharing and reviews

  • Voice coaching: spoken cues are generated on your device by iOS speech synthesis. The App never uses the microphone and never records audio.
  • Share cards: when you reach a milestone you can create a share image on your device. It is shared only if you choose a destination in the iOS share sheet, and it contains only the milestone and Day number shown on it.
  • Review prompts: after a stage pass, the App may ask iOS to show Apple's standard rating prompt. Apple handles any rating you give.

6. Advertising measurement

We promote First Rep with Apple Search Ads. Campaign results (such as installs from a keyword) are reported to us by Apple in aggregate through Apple's own advertising tools. The App itself does not include any advertising or attribution SDK, does not request the IDFA, and does not track you across apps or websites. If we add a privacy-preserving attribution method in the future, we will update this Policy before it ships.

7. What we never collect

  • No name, email, phone number or account details.
  • No location, contacts, photos, calendar, camera or microphone access.
  • No Apple Health data. The App does not read from or write to Health.
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA) and no behavioural profiles.
  • No sale, rental or sharing of personal information, under any circumstances.

8. How the information is used

The on-device information described above is used only to:

  • Show the right Day, set targets from your own results, and decide when your next Day is ready.
  • Judge stage tests and milestones from the sets you log.
  • Show your progress, records and history.
  • Pause your plan after pain and ease you back in safely.
  • Schedule the reminders you allow.
  • Unlock First Rep Pro when Apple confirms your subscription.

10. Storage, security and retention

Storage: training data lives in the App's private sandbox on your iPhone and is encrypted by iOS data protection when your device is locked after a restart.

Retention: data stays on your device until you erase it with Reset programme or delete the App. If your subscription ends, your history and records stay available on your device.

11. Your rights and controls

  • Erase your data: You › Settings › Reset programme erases every Day, set and recovery record on your iPhone. This cannot be undone.
  • Notifications: turn reminders off in Settings in the App, or in iOS Settings › Notifications › First Rep.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel in iOS Settings › [Your Name] › Subscriptions.
  • Questions or requests: because we do not hold your training data, we cannot access, export or delete it for you, but we are happy to help with any question. Contact us below.

12. European privacy rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port personal data, and to complain to your supervisory authority. Because First Rep processes your training data only on your own device and the Developer receives none of it, these rights can be exercised directly in the App (for example, by erasing your data). For any question, email inandha97@gmail.com.

13. California privacy rights (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion and correction, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not collect, sell or share your personal information. Contact us with any request.

14. Age requirement and children

First Rep is designed for adults aged 18 and over and asks you to confirm your age during setup. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child is using the App, contact us.

15. Changes to this Policy

If we change how the App handles information, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date before the change takes effect. Significant changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.
