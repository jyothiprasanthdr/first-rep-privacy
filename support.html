Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription? On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose PullPath Pro and tap Cancel. You can also use Manage on the PullPath Pro card in the app's Settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before a free trial or renewal ends to avoid being charged.

How do I restore my purchase? In the app open Settings and tap Restore purchases, or tap Restore on the subscription screen.

How do I delete my account and data? In the app open Settings, then Your data, then Delete account and data. This removes your Apple sign-in link and erases every Day, record and setting stored on your iPhone. It does not cancel your subscription; cancel that as described above.
